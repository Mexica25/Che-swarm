## Objection Responses

# Woody's Tree Xperts — Objection Handling Guide
### Face-to-Face Closing Scripts for Field Reps

---

## 1. 💰 Price Objection

**"That's more than I expected. I think that's a little too expensive."**

I completely understand — tree work is one of those things you don't budget for every day, and I never want anyone to feel caught off guard. What I can tell you is that price includes everything: the crew, equipment, cleanup, and full liability coverage so your property is protected if anything goes sideways. Can we look at what matters most to you today and make sure you're getting exactly what you need?

---

## 2. ⏰ Timing Objection

**"I'm just not ready right now. Maybe I'll call you back in a few months."**

I totally respect that — and I'd rather you feel confident than rushed. The one thing I'd hate for you is to wait on a tree that's already showing stress, especially heading into storm season here in North Carolina, because a damaged tree doesn't wait on our schedule. If I could lock in today's pricing and get you on the calendar for a date that works *for you*, would that take the pressure off?

---

## 3. 🔨 DIY / Friend Objection

**"I think my son-in-law can handle it. He's got a chainsaw and some free time."**

I love that he's willing to help — that says a lot about your family. The honest truth is that tree removal is one of the top causes of serious injury for homeowners, and without the right equipment and training, even a small job can go wrong fast. If something happened, you'd both be dealing with a lot more than a tree — let us take that risk off the table for you.

---

## 4. 🏆 Competition Objection

**"I like to do my due diligence. I'm going to get a couple more quotes before I decide."**

That is absolutely the smart move, and I'd encourage it — you should feel great about who you hire to work on your property. What I'd ask you to look for in those other quotes is proof of insurance, how long they've been operating locally, and whether cleanup is included, because a lower number doesn't always mean a lower cost when you factor all that in. If everything checks out and we're the right fit, would you be comfortable making a decision by the end of the week?

---

## 5. 👫 Approval Objection

**"I need to run this by my spouse before I commit to anything."**

That makes complete sense — this is your home and you should absolutely be on the same page. Would it help if I put together a written estimate with everything spelled out so they have all the details in front of them tonight? And if it's easier, I'm happy to come back when you're both here so I can answer any questions together — I just want to make sure neither of you feels like you're missing anything before you decide.

---

*Remember: Every objection is a request for more confidence. Acknowledge, reframe, and always ask a closing question.*
