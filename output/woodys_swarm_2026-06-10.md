## Objection Responses

# Woody's Tree Xperts — Objection Responses for the Field
### Close More Deals Face-to-Face

---

## 1. 💰 Price Objection

**"That's more than I expected. I wasn't planning to spend that much."**

I completely understand — tree work isn't something most people budget for, and that number can catch you off guard. What I can tell you is that price reflects a fully licensed, insured crew with professional equipment, so if anything goes wrong near your home, *you're protected.* Can we look at what matters most to you and make sure we're putting your money exactly where it counts?

---

## 2. ⏰ Timing Objection

**"I'm just not ready right now. Maybe I'll call you back in the fall."**

I hear you, and I never want to pressure anyone — but I do want to be honest with you as a neighbor. Trees don't wait on our schedules, and a compromised limb or root system can become a much bigger problem — or a much bigger bill — by fall. If we can lock in your spot today, I can also guarantee today's pricing before our summer rate adjustment kicks in.

---

## 3. 🪚 DIY / Third-Party Objection

**"My son-in-law said he can take care of it. He's pretty handy."**

That's great that you've got someone willing to help — family showing up like that says a lot. I just want to make sure he knows what he's getting into, because tree removal is one of the leading causes of serious injury for non-professionals, especially near a structure like your roof or fence. If something goes sideways, *there's no insurance covering it* — and that's a tough spot to put your son-in-law in. Let us take that risk off the table for him and for you.

---

## 4. 🏆 Competition Objection

**"I want to get a couple more quotes before I make a decision."**

That's a smart way to shop, and I respect it — you should feel confident in who you hire. Here's what I'd ask you to compare apples to apples on: licensing, liability insurance, and whether cleanup is included, because those are often where cheaper quotes cut corners. If after those conversations Woody's still makes the most sense — and I think we will — I'd love to earn your business.

---

## 5. 👫 Approval Objection

**"I need to run this by my husband/wife before I can commit to anything."**

Absolutely — a decision like this should be made together, and I wouldn't want it any other way. Would it help if I put together a written estimate you can both review tonight, so you're walking in with all the details in hand? And if it's easier, I'm happy to come back at a time when you're both home — even 15 minutes together can answer every question and get you taken care of fast.

---

*🌲 Remember: Confidence closes. Empathy keeps the door open. Always ask for the next step.*
