## 1. 💰 PRICE
**"Wow, that's more than I expected. That seems really expensive."**

I completely understand — tree work looks simple from the outside, and sticker shock is real. What that price covers is full liability insurance, certified arborists, and complete cleanup so your yard looks better than when we arrived. Compared to one emergency room visit or a roof repair from a fallen limb, this investment today is the cheaper option — want me to walk you through exactly what's included line by line?

---

## 2. ⏰ TIMING
**"I'm just not ready right now. Maybe I'll call you back in a few months."**

I hear you, and I never want to rush anyone into a decision. I do want to be honest with you though — that split in the trunk we looked at doesn't get safer over time, especially heading into storm season here in North Carolina. If we can get you on the schedule this week, we can lock in today's pricing and you won't have to think about it again — does Thursday or Friday work better for your family?

---

## 3. 🔨 DIY
**"I think my son-in-law can probably just take care of it. He's pretty handy."**

That's great that you have someone willing to help — that says a lot. The honest truth is that tree removal is one of the top causes of serious injury for non-professionals, and once something goes wrong near a house or power line, it happens fast. We'd hate to see your son-in-law get hurt or end up with a bigger repair bill — let us take the risk so he stays a hero at the family cookout instead.

---

## 4. 🏆 COMPETITION
**"I always get a few quotes before I decide. I'm going to shop around a little."**

That's a smart way to buy — you should absolutely feel confident in who you hire. I'll just tell you what separates us: Woody's carries full workers' comp and liability, we're locals who'll still be here if anything needs to be addressed after the job, and our crew treats your yard like it's our own. If a competitor comes in lower, ask them those same questions — and if the answers hold up, you'll know you made the right call either way.

---

## 5. 👫 APPROVAL
**"I really need to run this by my husband/wife before I commit to anything."**

Absolutely — a decision like this should be made together, that makes total sense. Would it help if I gave you both a call tonight, or even swung back by for 15 minutes so your spouse can ask questions directly and see the estimate firsthand? That way nobody's making a big decision on secondhand information, and you two can feel good about it together.

---

*💡 Pro Tip: Always end every response with a low-pressure question that moves toward a next step — a date, a callback, or a follow-up visit. Keep the door open and keep the momentum.*
