As a homeowner or property manager, post-storm tree assessment isn't just a landscaping concern — it's a liability concern.

Structurally compromised trees following high-wind events and saturated soil conditions represent one of the most underreported hazards in residential and commercial property management. The damage isn't always visible from the ground. Split unions, internal decay exposed by storm stress, and root destabilization require a trained eye and proper equipment to identify accurately.

This is the core value of working with **certified arborists**: not just removal, but risk assessment. Knowing *which* trees are dangerous, *why* they're dangerous, and what intervention is appropriate — whether that's full removal, crown reduction, or cabling and bracing.

## Objection Responses

# Woody's Tree Xperts — Objection Handling Guide
### Face-to-Face Closing Responses for Field Crews & Sales Reps

---

## 1. 💰 PRICE
**"That's more than I expected. I think that's a little too expensive."**

I completely understand — tree work is one of those things people don't budget for until they need it. What you're really paying for is a licensed, insured crew that protects your home, your yard, and your liability if something goes wrong. Can we look at what's most urgent today and find a starting point that works for your budget?

---

## 2. ⏰ TIMING
**"I'm not ready right now. Maybe I'll call you later in the summer."**

That's totally fair, and I respect that. I do want to mention that storm season is already here, and a compromised tree can become an emergency fast — and emergency calls cost significantly more than scheduled work. If I can get you on the calendar now, I can lock in today's price and you won't have to worry about it hanging over you.

---

## 3. 🪚 DIY
**"My son-in-law said he could probably take care of it for me."**

That's great that you have someone willing to help — that says a lot. The honest truth is that tree removal is one of the most dangerous home jobs there is, and without the right equipment, even experienced guys can get hurt or damage the property. If something goes wrong, you're on the hook — and I'd hate for a favor to turn into a much bigger problem for your family.

---

## 4. 🏆 COMPETITION
**"I want to get a couple more quotes before I make a decision."**

Absolutely — that's a smart way to approach any home project, and I'd expect nothing less. Just make sure whoever you're comparing us to is fully licensed and insured in North Carolina, because a lot of lowball quotes cut corners there and leave *you* liable. If Woody's is in the same ballpark, is there any reason we wouldn't be the right team for the job?

---

## 5. 👫 APPROVAL
**"I really need to run this by my husband/wife before I commit to anything."**

Of course — big decisions like this should absolutely be made together, I completely get that. Would it help if I called or stopped back when you're both available so I can answer any questions they might have directly? That way nobody's playing telephone, and you two can decide together with all the facts in front of you.

---

*Remember: Always end with a question. Control the next step. Never leave without a scheduled follow-up.*
